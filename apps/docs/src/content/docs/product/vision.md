House Radar is a real estate discovery and analysis platform. It centralizes listings from different sources, normalizes their data, and lets users search, compare, and track properties from a single application.

## Initial scope

The recommended initial scope is **Galicia**, starting with the **Santiago de Compostela** area.

## MVP goal

Have a real estate database of its own, fed through authorized sources, with:

- an advanced search,
- property detail pages,
- a map,
- favorites,
- and price history.

## Project purpose

House Radar is a personal tool built to real-product standards. It serves as a portfolio piece and may evolve into a public product (see [D0](/adr/d00-project-purpose/)). Product quality is expected from the start: ADRs, tests, CI, and observability. The MVP scope stays bounded: one user, Galicia, one source.
