# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference. The full text at that link applies to everyone taking part in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space related to it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or restrict participation, when behavior is inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@JSisques](https://github.com/JSisques)). For sensitive matters, open a private security advisory on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and describe the conduct issue there. Reports are handled in confidence.

Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
