---
title: Product principles
description: The seven principles that guide every product decision in House Radar.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. **The own database is the main asset.** Every new listing, price change, and update must add value to the House Radar history.
2. **Separate property and listing.** A real property can have several listings on different portals.
3. **Preserve provenance.** Every piece of data must be traceable to its source and observation date.
4. **Do not invent information.** Unknown values must remain unknown; features must not be inferred without saying so.
5. **Design for multiple sources.** Connectors must be independent and produce normalized data.
6. **Prioritize usefulness over AI.** First build a good database and a reliable search.
7. **Respect source terms.** Ingestion must rely on APIs, feeds, permissions, or other authorized collection methods.
